Screen reader users: the maze takes arrow keys while you play. With NVDA, if arrows don't move you, press NVDA and Space to switch to focus mode.

The sounds

Each sound has one meaning, and it keeps that meaning in every level.

  • Step You moved one square.
  • Wall There's a wall that way. You didn't move.
  • Fork More than one way is open here.
  • Soft Listening down this path: it leads out.
  • Harsh Listening down this path: it doesn't lead out.
  • Way out You've reached the exit.
  • Up Route tone: high.
  • Down Route tone: low.
  • Left Route tone: two short notes, from the left.
  • Right Route tone: one long note, from the right.
  • No listens You've used all your listens for this level.

Sound Maze

The maze is hidden while you play. You'll see it when you get out.

Score

Out of 100. You lose 1 point for each step beyond the shortest route (40 at most), 4 for each listen and 2 for each route replay. Touching walls costs nothing.
